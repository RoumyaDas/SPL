**Matchday 10 Summary – SPL Season 09**

Matchday 10 delivered four games, and the theme was clear: bowlers who struck early and batters who held their nerve. Here's how it all unfolded.

---

**Match 46: RR vs SRH – SRH won by 6 wickets (11 balls remaining)**

RR chose to bat and built a strong 207/8, powered by KA Pollard's blistering 63 off 31 and GD Phillips' 42 off 26. NT Ellis was the standout for SRH with 3/38, while SP Narine chipped in with 2/45. In reply, SRH's chase was masterful. P Simran Singh smashed 88 off 39 (10 fours, 4 sixes) and KL Rahul anchored with 86 off 47, as SRH cruised to 210/4 in just 18.1 overs. RR's bowlers had no answers, with A Nagwaswalla (2/31) the only multiple wicket-taker. P Simran Singh took Player of the Match honors.

---

**Match 47: RCB vs DC – DC won by 7 wickets (22 balls remaining)**

DC won the toss and elected to bowl first on a pace-friendly surface, and JJ Bumrah made it count with a sensational 5/34, ripping through RCB's top order. RCB limped to 166/9, with Abhishek Pathak's 47 off 32 the only real resistance. Easwaran K supported Bumrah with 2/21. In the chase, DC lost early wickets but N Sindhu played a stunning unbeaten 78 off 37 (8 fours, 3 sixes) to take control, well supported by GJ Maxwell's 38 off 27. DC reached 167/3 in 16.1 overs. S Gill picked up 2/36 for RCB, but it wasn't enough. JJ Bumrah was named Player of the Match.

---

**Match 48: CSK vs GT – GT won by 1 wicket (5 balls remaining)**

A thriller at CSK. GT won the toss and bowled first. CSK posted 201/9, with Tanush Kotian's rapid 37 off 17 (4 sixes) and Tilak Varma's 48 off 27 leading the charge. Fazalhaq Farooqi was excellent for GT with 3/27, and B Muzarabani took 2/22. In a tense chase, GT were powered by JP Inglis' 61 off 33 and V Nigam's explosive 54 off 21. But the story of the match was Geet Puri, whose 5/43 nearly won it for CSK. GT held their nerve to reach 202/9 in 19.1 overs, sealing a one-wicket win. Geet Puri was named Player of the Match despite being on the losing side.

---

**Match 49: PBKS vs KKR – KKR won by 15 runs**

KKR batted first after winning the toss and posted 144/4, with TM Head carrying his bat for an unbeaten 73 off 61. YS Samra contributed 34 off 31. PBKS's Shashank Singh was the pick of the bowlers with 2/22, while AR Ramjani was economical with 1/15. In reply, PBKS collapsed dramatically. Shahbaz Ahmed was the destroyer-in-chief with 4/14, and D Wiese took 3/11 as PBKS were bowled out for 129 in 15.2 overs. AR Patel top-scored with 39 off 24, but it was never enough. Shahbaz Ahmed was named Player of the Match.

---

**Match 50: MI vs LSG – MI won by 32 runs**

On a spin-friendly track at MI, LSG won the toss and bowled first. WL Madsen played a lone hand for MI, carrying his bat for a magnificent 94 off 55 (13 fours, 1 six) as MI reached 160/8. DS Rathi and H Ssenyondo both took 3 wickets for LSG. In the chase, LSG were never allowed to settle. Satyajeet Bachhav was unplayable with 4/19, and Shivam Shukla took 3/27 as LSG were bowled out for 128 in 16.5 overs. Sameer Rizvi fought with an unbeaten 47 off 27, but wickets tumbled around him. WL Madsen was named Player of the Match for his match-winning knock.

---

**Matchday 10 Roundup:** Five games, five decisive results. SRH, DC, GT, KKR, and MI all walked away with wins, while RR, RCB, CSK, PBKS, and LSG were left to regroup. The day belonged to bowlers like Bumrah, Bachhav, and Shahbaz Ahmed, and to batters like Madsen, P Simran Singh, and N Sindhu who stood tall under pressure.
