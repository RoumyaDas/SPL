## 🔥 The Big Storylines

**Chasing was a graveyard.** 3 of five teams batting second fell short — only MI and SRH completed successful chases, and both did it with wickets to spare. RR, DC, and GT all defended totals successfully.

**Spin ruled Matchday 03.** Match 12 (RCB vs SRH) had a 3.5x spin assist, Match 13 (RR vs CSK) had 3.0x, and Match 15 (PBKS vs GT) had 2.0x. The only pace-friendly deck was Match 14 (LSG vs DC) at 4.0x — and that game saw Mohammad Amir and JJ Bumrah combine for 8 wickets.

**The POTM curse was real.** Three of five Player of the Match awards went to players from the *losing* side — S Arora (KKR), CH Gayle (RCB), and Tanush Kotian (CSK) all starred in defeat.

## 📊 Match-by-Match in One Line

**KKR vs MI:** KKR posted 221 on the back of S Arora's 94, but MI hunted it down with 2 balls to spare — MJ Owen's 62 and Jay Gohil's 39* (13) sealing a 7-wicket win.

**RCB vs SRH:** CH Gayle's lone hand of 73 dragged RCB to 144, but SRH's chase of 148 was never in doubt — FH Allen's 42 and Karan Shinde's 36 finishing the job with 14 balls left.

**RR vs CSK:** RR's 217/5 (Aniket Verma 54, S Dube 71) proved 25 runs too many — Tanush Kotian's 54 for CSK wasn't enough as RR's bowlers (Karan Lal 2/34, Ghazanfar 2/41) held their nerve.

**LSG vs DC:** DC's 220/9 (N Sindhu 76, GJ Maxwell 54) was built on Amir's 4/33 — but LSG's chase crumbled to 189 all out, JJ Bumrah's 4/27 (10 dots) the difference in a 31-run DC win.

**PBKS vs GT:** GT's 188/6 (Naman Dhir 57, V Nigam 45) looked par — and it was, as PBKS fell 4 runs short despite PD Salt's 52 and Vansh Bedi's 47. GF Linde and R Sai Kishore took 3 wickets each.
