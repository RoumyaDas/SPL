SPL S09 • QUALIFIER 02 • PBKS vs SRH • SRH

oss: SRH won, elected to bowl first
Venue: SRH
Target: 210

SRH won the toss and chose to bowl, and for a while it looked inspired — Zampa and Narine each struck twice, and PBKS were wobbling at 87/4 inside ten overs. But then came the counterpunch: RK Singh (65 off 32) and Shashank Singh (53 off 30) tore into the death bowling, and PBKS stormed to 209/6 — a total that felt 15 runs above par.

SRH's chase began like a hurricane. P Simran Singh and FH Allen obliterated the powerplay — 76/0 in six — and the target seemed a formality. Allen fell for a brilliant 77 (47) at 154/1 in the 15th, and the equation tightened. KL Rahul fell for 1, Iyer for 11, Kartik Sharma for 6 — wickets tumbled as the required rate climbed.

But Simran Singh stood immovable, carrying his bat for a majestic **105* off 59**. Needing 21 off the last over, he smashed 16 — four, dot, six, four — but it wasn't enough. SRH finished 205/4, four runs short. PBKS survived a scare for the ages; Simran Singh's century was in vain.
