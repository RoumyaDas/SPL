# SPL S09 – Matchday 07 Summary

Matchday 07 delivered four thrilling contests, with three games going down to the wire and one absolute demolition job. Here's everything that unfolded:

---

## Match 31: CSK vs PBKS
**Venue:** CSK | **Toss:** PBKS won, chose to bowl

CSK posted a formidable **202/7** on the back of a blistering death-overs assault. **Kranthi Kumar** (46 off 24) and **T Stubbs** (38 off 24) tore into the PBKS bowling, while **JJ Smit** (3/28) was the pick of the bowlers. In reply, PBKS came out all guns blazing — **V Suryavanshi** smashed a 25-ball **63** (5 fours, 5 sixes) to give his side a flying start. But once he fell, the chase lost momentum despite **PD Salt**'s 33. CSK's bowlers, led by **Geet Puri** (2/42) and **M Pathirana** (2/39), squeezed the life out of the innings. PBKS finished on **192 all out**, falling short by 10 runs. **V Suryavanshi** was named Player of the Match despite being on the losing side — a testament to his explosive knock.

**Result:** CSK won by 10 runs.

---

## Match 32: SRH vs DC
**Venue:** SRH_2 | **Toss:** SRH won, chose to bat

The closest game of the matchday. SRH piled on **228/4**, with **KL Rahul** (67 off 41) and **FH Allen** (60 off 39) setting the platform before **VR Iyer** (29* off 11) provided a brutal finish. DC's chase was built on a sensational 45-ball **81** from **Tarang Gohel** and a 34-ball **66** from **SA Yadav**. At 167/2 in the 15th over, DC looked favourites. But SRH's bowlers — particularly **Tripuresh Singh** (2/34) and **A Zampa** (1/27) — pulled things back. **AD Russell** nearly pulled off a heist with a stunning **29* off 8** (1 four, 3 sixes), but DC fell agonisingly short by **1 run** at **227/5**. **Tarang Gohel** was deservedly Player of the Match for his match-winning effort in a losing cause.

**Result:** SRH won by 1 run.

---

## Match 33: KKR vs GT
**Venue:** KKR | **Toss:** GT won, chose to bat

A one-sided affair from start to finish. GT limped to **152/8**, with **JP Inglis** (29) and **Mangesh Yadav** (29) the only batters to pass 25. **MR Marsh** (3/35) and **MA Wood** (2/41) were relentless. In reply, KKR openers **Tushar Raheja** (80* off 38) and **Ishan Kishan** (72* off 37) produced an absolute masterclass, chasing down the target in just **12.3 overs** without losing a single wicket. GT's bowlers had no answers — not a single wicket fell, and the required rate never exceeded 7.5. **Tushar Raheja** was Player of the Match for his brutal 10-four, 3-six knock.

**Result:** KKR won by 10 wickets.

---

## Match 34: RCB vs MI
**Venue:** RCB | **Toss:** MI won, chose to bowl

The highest-scoring thriller of the day. RCB recovered from 10/3 to post **212/8**, thanks to **Priyansh Arya** (69 off 43) and **Abhishek Pathak** (62 off 33). **Abhilash Shetty** (3/42) and **JR Hazlewood** (2/40) kept MI in check. MI's chase was a rollercoaster — **Urvil Patel** (63 off 31) and **Jay Gohil** (54 off 22) put them ahead, but RCB kept striking. At 203/8, MI needed 10 off the last two overs. **MW Short** (22* off 7) held his nerve, and MI crossed the line with **9 wickets down** and **11 balls to spare**. **S Gill** was Player of the Match for his all-round contribution (32 off 11 with the bat, 2/41 with the ball) — a rare instance of a POTM award going to a player on the losing side.

**Result:** MI won by 1 wicket.

---

## Match 35: RR vs LSG
**Venue:** RR | **Toss:** RR won, chose to bat

On a spin-friendly surface (assisting spin 2.6x more than pace), RR scrapped their way to **164/9**. **WG Jacks** (43* off 32) held the innings together, while **S Dube** (31 off 24) provided support. **H Ssenyondo** (3/26) and **DS Rathi** (3/49) were excellent for LSG. The chase never got going — **Karan Lal** struck with the very first ball to remove **Shubman Gill** for a golden duck, and LSG's middle order crumbled. **Ajay Rohera** (63 off 30) fought valiantly, but **Utkarsh Singh** (3/24) ran through the lower order. LSG were bowled out for **140** in 18.2 overs. **Utkarsh Singh** was Player of the Match for his match-winning spell.

**Result:** RR won by 24 runs.
