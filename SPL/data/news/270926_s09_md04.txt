## 🔥 Match 16: CSK vs SRH – The Narine Show

**SRH 199/10 (19.5) | CSK 192/5 (20.0) | SRH won by 7 runs**

A spin-heavy pitch (2.1x) promised a battle of the slow bowlers, and it delivered. CSK's decision to bowl first looked smart early as **RE van der Merwe (3/40)** and **Arshad Khan (3/41)** ripped through SRH's top order. But **SP Narine (42 off 24)** and **KH Pandya (42 off 19)** rescued the innings with brutal hitting — Narine's four sixes and Pandya's five fours and three sixes pushed SRH to a competitive 199.

CSK's chase was built on **T Stubbs' unbeaten 53 (31)** and **KK Nair's 37 (22)**, but **M Markande (2/24)** and **A Zampa (2/25)** choked the middle overs. Narine capped his all-round day with 1/38, and CSK fell 7 runs short despite a late **RA Jadeja cameo (19 off 8)**.

> **The moment:** Narine's 42 off 24 — every ball seemed to find the boundary when SRH needed it most.

---

## 🔥 Match 17: RR vs MI – Dube's Dominance & Curran's Carnage

**RR 185/7 (20.0) | MI 117/10 (16.4) | RR won by 68 runs**

A 3.3x spin-assist pitch at RR, and **S Dube** played the innings of the day — **87* off 56** with seven fours and four sixes. RR stumbled early (**23/2 in 1.3 overs**), but Dube rebuilt with **YBK Jaiswal (35 off 22)** and then exploded in the death overs, smashing **S Lamichhane for three consecutive sixes** in the 19th over.

MI's chase never got going. **Shivam Shukla (3/15, 16 dots!)** produced a miserly spell that suffocated the top order, while **Mohammad Ghazanfar (3/34)** and **SM Curran (4/33)** cleaned up the rest. Only **Urvil Patel (39 off 20)** offered resistance. MI collapsed from **62/2 to 117 all out** — a staggering loss of 8 wickets for 55 runs.

> **The moment:** Curran's 4/33 — the death overs were a procession.

---

## 🔥 Match 18: KKR vs DC – Archer's Ashes-Style Masterclass

**KKR 155/10 (18.3) | DC 134/10 (16.3) | KKR won by 21 runs**

A 4.0x pace-friendly pitch at KKR, and **JC Archer** delivered a spell for the ages — **4/21 from 4 overs with 13 dots**. **JJ Bumrah (3/18)** was equally lethal as KKR's batting lineup folded for 155 despite **Ishan Kishan's 42 (27)** and **C Green's 33 (23)**.

DC's chase was a disaster. **Shahbaz Ahmed (4/39)** and **Suryansh Shedge (3/27, 12 dots)** tore through the order. **GJ Maxwell (41 off 22)** and **SA Yadav (36 off 28)** gave brief hope, but once Maxwell was run out by Shahbaz, the innings imploded — **from 86/2 to 134 all out**. Archer's all-round day (4/21 plus a crucial catch) sealed a dominant KKR win.

> **The moment:** Archer's 13 dots — DC simply couldn't get him away.

---

## 🔥 Match 19: PBKS vs RCB – The 200 vs 202 Thriller

**RCB 200/6 (20.0) | PBKS 202/5 (16.3) | PBKS won by 5 wickets**

The highest-scoring game of the day. On a 2.0x spin-assist pitch, **BJ McMullen** played a lone hand for RCB with **87 off 61** (5 fours, 4 sixes), well supported by **PWH de Silva's explosive 32* off 11**. **R Ashwin (3/20)** was the pick of PBKS bowlers, but RCB's 200 looked imposing.

PBKS, however, made a mockery of the target. **Vansh Bedi** produced a stunning **80 off 34** (8 fours, 5 sixes) — including a **32-run over off PWH de Silva** that turned the game on its head. **TP Ura (43 off 26)** played the perfect supporting act, and PBKS romped home in just **16.3 overs**. RCB's bowlers had no answers — **CH Gayle (2/36)** and **KA Maharaj (2/35)** were the only wicket-takers, but the economy rates told the story.

> **The moment:** Vansh Bedi's 32-run assault on PWH de Silva — the over that broke RCB's back.

---

## 🔥 Match 20: LSG vs GT – Ssenyondo Spins LSG to Victory

**LSG 187/8 (20.0) | GT 140/10 (18.2) | LSG won by 47 runs**

A 3.0x pace-assist pitch at LSG_2, and **Shubman Gill** produced a batting masterclass — **59 off 32** with 10 fours. **Abishek Porel (31 off 20)** and **HH Pandya (21 off 12)** provided late fireworks, while **Prince Yadav (3/44)** and **Fazalhaq Farooqi (2/12, 16 dots!)** kept LSG to 187.

GT's chase was all about **Naman Dhir (51 off 34)** and **SV Samson (37 off 35)** — but once **H Ssenyondo** entered the attack, the game changed. The left-arm spinner took **3/23 from 3.2 overs**, including the crucial wickets of **V Nigam (3)**, **Ashutosh Sharma (8)**, and **Fazalhaq Farooqi (0)**. **Mohammad Amir (2/29)** and **Arshdeep Singh (2/30)** provided excellent support as GT collapsed from **97/4 to 140 all out**.

> **The moment:** Ssenyondo's double-wicket over (18.1–18.2) — Ashutosh bowled, Farooqi caught, game over.
