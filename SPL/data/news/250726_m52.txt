# 🏏 SPL S08, Match 52: RR vs GT – A Run-Fest for the Ages

If you love sixes, this was your paradise. RR won the toss and sent GT in to bat on a track that offered true bounce for both spinners and pacers. The decision backfired almost immediately as GT's openers went berserk.

SV Samson started cautiously before JP Inglis launched a brutal assault, smashing 55 off just 23 balls. The duo added 79 in the powerplay, setting the platform for absolute carnage. Naman Dhir continued the charge with a quick 40, but the real damage came from D Brevis and the explosive R Parag. The pair added an unbeaten 91 runs off just 46 balls, with Parag smashing 58 off 25 and Brevis contributing a composed 56 off 31. GT amassed a mammoth 241/3, the highest total of the season.

Chasing 242, RR were dealt a massive blow on the very first ball when Aniket Verma was bowled for a duck. But KA Pollard, playing against his former franchise, anchored the innings with a gritty 71, while Kumar Kushagra played the innings of his life, scoring a brilliant 72. The pair added 137 runs for the third wicket, keeping RR in the hunt. However, the required rate climbed past 18, and despite a late cameo from GD Phillips (40 off 14), RR fell short by 16 runs. GT's bowlers held their nerve, with GF Linde getting the early breakthrough and Sufiyan Muqeem picking the crucial wicket of Pollard.

A record-breaking batting display from GT proved too much for RR, who fought valiantly but couldn't chase down the mountain. GT wins by 16 runs.
