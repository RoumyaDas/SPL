**🏏 SPL S08 – QUALIFIER 2: DC vs KKR – A CHASE FOR THE AGES**

In a high-stakes Qualifier 2 that had everything, KKR pulled off a record-breaking chase to book their place in the final. It was a night of breathtaking hitting and nerve-shredding drama.

DC, put in to bat, got off to a rocky start losing Maxwell early, but N Sindhu played a captain's knock of 75 off 41 balls, anchoring the innings. Ritik Vats provided the perfect finishing touch with an explosive 71* off 39, while Andre Russell's cameo (49 off 19) propelled DC to a massive 234/5. The target looked ominous.

But KKR's openers had other plans. Ishan Kishan and TM Head put on a masterclass, tearing into the DC bowling attack. Kishan's blistering 83 off 37 and Head's fluent 63 off 32 laid the perfect foundation. Despite a mid-innings wobble that saw quick wickets, KKR never lost their composure. Some late hitting from S Arora and Anmolpreet Singh sealed the chase with 9 balls to spare, completing one of the most thrilling run-chases of the season.

It was a chase that showcased KKR's firepower, determination, and belief. They now march into the final, having conquered a monumental target with style and authority.

**Winner:** KKR (Won by 5 Wickets)  
**Player of the Match:** Ishan Kishan (KKR)
