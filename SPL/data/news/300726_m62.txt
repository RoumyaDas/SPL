# 🏏 SPL Season 08, Match 62 – CSK vs RR

A spin-friendly track that produced a run-fest, RR pulled off a heist off the final ball to stun CSK in a nail-biter.

CSK, invited to bat, lost early wickets, but **Tilak Varma** played a masterful anchor knock of 98 off 57 balls. He found able support from **KK Nair** (56 off 28) and a late blitz from **Tanush Kotian** (32 off 10) that propelled CSK to 223/4 — a total that looked imposing on a turning track.

RR's chase started steady but stumbled to 75/3. Then came the counter-attack. **RD Rickelton** anchored with 51 off 30, **GD Phillips** smashed 43 off 23, and when the equation seemed impossible, **YBK Jaiswal** walked in and produced an absolute masterpiece — 51* off just 14 balls, including 4 sixes and 6 fours. With 20 needed off the last over, Jaiswal sealed the chase with a six off the final delivery, giving RR a thrilling last-ball victory.

A classic that went down to the wire — RR held their nerve in a high-pressure finish.
