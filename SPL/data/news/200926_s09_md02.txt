🏏 **SPL S09 – MATCHDAY 02: WHERE BATTERS RULED AND CHASES WENT WILD** 🏏

Matchday 02 was a pure spectacle of power-hitting, last-over heists, and one innings that defied belief. Five matches. Ten innings. And a mountain of runs that left bowlers searching for answers.

**MI vs DC** – A pace-friendly deck did nothing to stop the carnage. DC recovered from a shaky start to post 187 thanks to Russell's late blitz, but MI's response was ruthless. MJ Owen produced a masterclass — an unbeaten 105 off 65 balls — and Abhishek Sharma played the perfect foil. MI cruised home with an over to spare, never once looking troubled.

**GT vs KKR** – Spin was the order of the day, and GF Linde feasted. His 3-wicket haul restricted KKR to 189 despite Ishan Kishan's blistering 74. GT's chase wobbled early, but Dewald Brevis anchored the innings with a composed 74*, and R Parag finished it off with flair. GT won with 13 balls remaining.

**RCB vs RR** – A tale of two innings. RCB were reeling at 19/3 before Abhishek Pathak played the innings of the matchday — a stunning 112 off 53 balls — to drag them to 190. But RR's chase was clinical. S Dube (63) and GD Phillips (39*) kept the scoreboard ticking, and despite a late wobble, RR crossed the line with 8 balls to spare.

**SRH vs PBKS** – An absolute heart-stopper. PBKS posted 199 thanks to Shashank Singh's unbeaten 51 and TL Seifert's 55. SRH's chase looked dead and buried at 138/5, needing 62 off 18 balls. Enter VR Iyer. He smashed an unbeaten 56 off just 22 balls, and Kartik Sharma's 56 kept them alive. SRH fell just 4 runs short — PBKS won by 3 runs in a thriller.

**LSG vs CSK** – The Tilak Varma show. CSK's opener smashed 86 off 47, and RD Gaikwad made 76, propelling CSK to 207. LSG's chase was in tatters at 102/5. But HH Pandya (54*) and DR Mousley (47) combined for a rescue act, and Shubhang Hegde finished it with a six. LSG won with 18 balls to spare.

Matchday 02 reminded everyone why SPL S09 is the most unpredictable league in the world. Bowlers, take cover.
