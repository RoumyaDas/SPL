**The Grand Finale – A Story of Fire and Ice**

The stage was set. The crowd was roaring. SRH — the fortress — witnessed a finale for the ages.

PBKS won the toss and chose to bowl. A brave call. A fatal one? Time would tell.

**Act I: The Abhishek Sharma Storm**

Urvil Patel started like a runaway train — 25 off 13, four boundaries in the very first over off Ngarava. But JJ Smit struck twice in quick succession: Akshat Raghuwanshi LBW for a duck, then Urvil Patel bowled for 25. MI wobbling at 31/2.

Enter Abhishek Sharma.

What followed was not batting. It was destruction. 94 runs. 36 balls. 11 fours. 5 sixes. He tore into Shashank Singh (20 in an over), dismantled AR Ramjani (two sixes in an over), and left CV Varun searching for answers.

WL Madsen played the perfect foil — 48 off 34, holding one end while Abhishek rained hell. The 138-run stand broke PBKS's spirit.

Q de Kock (25 off 12) and Jay Gohil (30* off 11) applied the finishing touches — 237/6. A total that felt like a mountain.

JJ Smit was the lone warrior for PBKS — 3/24, 15 dot balls. But even he couldn't stop the tide.

**Act II: The Chase That Promised**

PBKS needed 238. V Suryavanshi and PD Salt said: "Why not?"

Suryavanshi was electric — 72 off 39, 8 fours, 2 sixes. He took on Abhishek Sharma and Sunil Kumar with disdain.

Salt was the enforcer — 65 off 36, 5 massive sixes. Together they kept PBKS alive, ticking at 12+ an over.

At 141/2 in the 12th over, PBKS were dreaming. The required rate was climbing, but Salt and Suryavanshi were finding boundaries at will.

**Act III: The Collapse**

Then Shivam Shukla struck the hammer blow. PD Salt — LBW. Gone for 65. The chase lost its pulse.

Satyajeet Bachhav, the quiet assassin, then ripped the heart out of PBKS: RK Singh caught for 14, V Suryavanshi caught for 72 — the big fish, AR Patel LBW for a golden duck.

164/6. The dream was dead.

Shashank Singh (20* off 11) fought bravely, AR Ramjani (12* off 9) swung hard, but 51 runs in the last two overs was too steep.

200/7. MI win by 37 runs.

**The Verdict**

Abhishek Sharma's 94 off 36 was the difference. A knock of pure violence, intelligence, and timing. He walked away with the Player of the Match award and the Grand Finale trophy in MI's hands.

PBKS fought. PBKS believed. But MI had a superhero.

MI — SPL S09 Champions.
