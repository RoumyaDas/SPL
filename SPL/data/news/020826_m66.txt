# 🏏 Match 66 – SRH vs RCB: A Bowling Masterclass and a Chase That Never Was

The stage was set for a high-voltage clash at the SRH_2, with the pitch offering significant assistance to pacers. SRH won the toss and elected to bat first, a decision that seemed justified as their openers laid a solid foundation. P Simran Singh played a fluent hand, while SU Parakh provided able support. However, wickets at regular intervals kept the RCB bowlers in the game. It was the late onslaught from KL Rahul, who anchored the innings with a brilliant 67, and VR Iyer, who provided the final flourish, that propelled SRH to an imposing total of 226. The total seemed well above par on a track that had something for the bowlers.

Chasing 227 for victory, RCB’s chase began with promise as their openers scored freely in the powerplay. But the introduction of the speedster Tripuresh Singh into the attack turned the game on its head. He was relentless, striking with every delivery and dismantling the RCB batting lineup. Wickets tumbled in a heap, and despite a fighting knock from PWH de Silva, the required run rate spiraled out of control. Tripuresh Singh’s phenomenal spell proved to be the difference, running through the RCB batting order and securing a comprehensive victory for SRH. It was a display of clinical bowling that left the opposition with no answers. 🏆

**Winner:** SRH  
**Player of the Match:** Tripuresh Singh
