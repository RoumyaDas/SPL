# 🏏 SPL Season 08, Match 63 – PBKS vs SRH

The pitch at PBKS was a spinner’s paradise, and it set the stage for a low-scoring thriller that swung like a pendulum until the very last over.

PBKS won the toss and opted to bat first, but the decision backfired early. **TL Seifert** departed in the second over, and **V Suryavanshi** followed soon after, trapped LBW by WG Jacks. **Vansh Bedi** played a blazing cameo of 38 off 18 balls, but his dismissal triggered a collapse that left PBKS reeling at 64/4. **Shashank Singh** held the innings together with a gritty 48* off 32 balls, but wickets kept tumbling at the other end. PBKS finished with a modest 148/9 in 20 overs — a total that seemed below par on a tricky surface.

In reply, SRH’s chase got off to a steady start. **P Simran Singh** anchored the innings with a well-crafted 54 off 43 balls, while **Kartik Sharma** played a stunning knock of 50* off 30 balls, taking the game away from PBKS. **KL Rahul** fell early, but the duo of Simran and Kartik added crucial runs under pressure. Shashank Singh tried to bring PBKS back with two wickets, but it wasn’t enough. SRH chased down the target with 9 balls to spare, winning by 6 wickets.

A game of small margins, where SRH held their nerve and PBKS paid for their batting collapse.
