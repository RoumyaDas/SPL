# 🏏 SPL S08, Match 53: SRH vs KKR – A Heart-Stopping Nail-Biter

On a pitch that heavily favored spin, KKR won the toss and elected to bat first, setting the stage for a high-octane clash.

KKR's openers, Ishan Kishan and TM Head, laid a rock-solid foundation, adding 125 runs for the first wicket. Kishan was fluent, scoring 66 off just 33 balls, while Head anchored the innings with a composed 83 off 62. The middle order then exploded – Cameron Green smashed an unbeaten 35 off a mere 10 balls, propelling KKR to an imposing 215/3.

Chasing 216, SRH lost an early wicket, but P Simran Singh played a captain's knock, scoring a magnificent century off just 58 balls. He found able support from JE Root (47) and Kartik Sharma (49*), keeping SRH in the hunt till the very end. Needing 22 off the last over, Kartik Sharma smashed a boundary and a six off the first two balls, bringing the equation down to 11 off 4 balls. However, some tight bowling and a crucial wicket left SRH agonizingly short at 214/4.

It was a thrilling contest that went down to the wire, but KKR held their nerve to snatch victory by just 1 run.
