# 🏏 SPL S08, Match 54: PBKS vs DC – A Chase for the Ages

DC won the toss and elected to bat first on a pitch that heavily favoured spin. The decision seemed to backfire initially as GJ Maxwell started aggressively before falling early to CV Varun. However, what followed was a masterclass from SA Yadav. He anchored the innings with a breathtaking unbeaten 114 off just 61 balls, smashing 8 sixes along the way. Parth Chandan played a perfect supporting role with a gritty 56, helping DC post a competitive total of 188/4. JJ Smit was the pick of the PBKS bowlers with 2 wickets.

Chasing 189, PBKS openers V Suryavanshi and Vansh Bedi launched a brutal assault. Suryavanshi smashed 60 off just 21 balls, including 6 sixes, while Bedi added a fluent 60 of his own. Despite a mini-collapse in the middle overs, PBKS were always ahead of the rate. The game turned dramatically when SP Narine walked in at number 8 with the scores level. He smashed 16 off just 3 balls, including two massive sixes, to seal the victory in style with 3 overs to spare.

It was a spectacular chase, with PBKS reaching the target in just 17 overs, showcasing sheer batting dominance over DC's bowling attack.
