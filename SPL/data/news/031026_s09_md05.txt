## 🔥 The Headlines

### 1. **DC's 262-Run Demolition** (DC vs RCB)
The highest total of Matchday 05 — and by some distance. **SA Yadav's 83* (33)** and **GJ Maxwell's 76 (34)** tore RCB apart in a display of pure T20 violence. Maxwell's 14 fours in the powerplay set the tone; Yadav's 5 sixes finished it. RCB fought hard through **Harnoor Singh's 80 (42)**, but **Harsh Dubey (3/40)** and **AK Markram (2/58)** kept the chase just out of reach.

### 2. **PD Salt's Century in Vain** (LSG vs PBKS)
Salt's **105 (63)** — 12 fours, 3 sixes — was the innings of the day, but it came in a losing cause. PBKS posted 196/8, and LSG chased it down with three balls to spare thanks to **Sameer Rizvi's 61* (27)** and **HH Pandya's 45* (21)**. A classic "scoreboard pressure" heist.

### 3. **The Tie That Felt Like a Win** (CSK vs MI)
**Akshat Raghuwanshi's 115* (62)** powered MI to 219/4. CSK needed 1 off the last ball — and finished on exactly 219/6. **Tilak Varma's 102 (55)** was the engine, and **RA Jadeja's 19* (7)** nearly stole it. Both teams walked away with a point, but MI will feel they let one slip.

### 4. **Finn Allen's Blitz** (KKR vs SRH)
Chasing 204, **FH Allen** smashed **81 (43)** with 5 sixes, and **P Simran Singh** anchored with **77* (53)** as SRH cruised home in **19 overs**. KKR's 203/8 — built on **TM Head's 45** and **Tushar Raheja's 34 (11)** — was never quite enough.

### 5. **Spin Chokes RR** (GT vs RR)
On a spin-friendly GT_2 surface, **GF Linde (3/20)**, **Sufiyan Muqeem (3/32)**, and **R Sai Kishore (3/46)** combined to bowl RR out for 162. **S Dube's 64 (31)** was the lone resistance. GT chased it down with **JP Inglis (49)** and **V Nigam's 15* (3)** finishing it off.
