# 🏏 SPL S08, Match 51: MI vs CSK – A High-Scoring Thriller at the Wankhede

The pitch was a spinner's paradise, and CSK captain won the toss, opting to bowl first—a decision that seemed golden for the first half of the match, but nearly backfired spectacularly.

MI's openers came out with pure intent. Urvil Patel, in blistering form, took charge from the very first over. He treated the CSK bowlers with utter disdain, smashing boundaries and sixes at will. He put on a rapid 38-run stand with Rohan Kunnummal before the latter fell. Abhishek Sharma joined the party and hit a six before being caught, but the real show was just beginning. Urvil Patel continued his masterclass, finding gaps and clearing the ropes with ease, bringing up a magnificent 89 off just 51 balls. Despite a mini-collapse in the death overs where they lost quick wickets, some late hitting from Arshad Khan (17 off 8) and a cameo from MR Marsh (14 off 9) pushed MI to a competitive total of 185/9. Prashant Solanki and Roelof van der Merwe were the pick of the CSK bowlers, each grabbing three wickets to restrict the home side.

Chasing 186, CSK openers V Kohli and JM Vince set the tone with a fiery start, punishing anything loose. Kohli smashed two sixes before being bowled by Satyajeet Bachhav. Vince held the innings together like an anchor, playing a composed yet aggressive knock, while Tilak Varma played a quick cameo of 24 to keep the required rate in check. The game turned when the in-form Sonu Yadav walked in. He launched a brutal assault on the MI bowlers, smashing 45 off just 24 balls with four fours and three massive sixes. His partnership with Vince virtually sealed the chase. Although Yadav fell, skipper T Stubbs walked in and finished the job with style, hitting the winning six to take CSK to victory with 7 balls to spare.

It was a nail-biting contest that went back and forth, but CSK's depth and calm finishing proved to be the difference on a tough pitch.
