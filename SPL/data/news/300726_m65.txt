# 🏏 SPL Season 08, Match 65 – LSG vs KKR

A spin-dominated contest that turned into a run-fest in the death overs, KKR pulled off a heist that left LSG shell-shocked.

LSG, batting first, lost early wickets but **Abishek Porel** held the innings together with a gritty 39 off 34 balls. **HH Pandya** and **Shubhang Hegde** provided the late fireworks, pushing LSG to 195/8. KKR's **Pulkit Narang** was the standout bowler, picking 4 crucial wickets and keeping LSG from breaching the 200-mark.

In reply, KKR lost both openers early, but **C Green** played a composed anchor knock of 66* off 47 balls, while **SS Iyer** chipped in with 53. With the required rate climbing past 20 in the final two overs, **Jay Gohil** walked in and played an astonishing cameo — 42* off just 12 balls, smashing 10 fours to turn the chase on its head. KKR chased down 196 with just 1 ball to spare.

A spectacular finish, where one man's madness stole the show.
