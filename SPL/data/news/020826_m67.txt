# 🏏 Match 67 – GT vs CSK: A Tale of Two Innings

The stage was set at GT_2 for a fascinating contest between GT and CSK, with the pitch offering significant assistance to pacers. CSK won the toss and elected to bowl first, a decision that initially seemed to work as they picked up early wickets. However, GT's innings was built around a gritty half-century from JP Inglis and crucial contributions from HC Brook and D Brevis. The middle order absorbed pressure and then exploded in the death overs, propelling GT to a competitive total of 193.

Chasing 194, CSK's innings never gained momentum. Their openers struggled to find the boundary, and the required run rate kept climbing. The GT bowling attack, led by the relentless duo of Mangesh Yadav and Prince Yadav, squeezed the life out of the chase. They picked up wickets at regular intervals, never allowing any CSK batter to settle. Tanush Kotian fought valiantly with a rapid 48, but the lack of support from the other end meant the target was always out of reach. GT's disciplined bowling display ensured they defended their total comfortably and secured a well-deserved victory. 🏆

**Winner:** GT  
**Player of the Match:** Mangesh Yadav
