# 🏏 SPL S08, Match 55: RCB vs LSG – A Spin-Fueled Run-Fest

LSG won the toss and elected to bowl first on a surface that heavily favored spin, but RCB's explosive batting line-up had other plans. After an early wicket, the legendary CH Gayle walked in and unleashed a vintage masterclass, smashing 79 off just 47 balls with 8 fours and 3 sixes. Priyansh Arya provided a quickfire start with 35 off 13, while Abhishek Pathak (36* off 9) and JM Sharma (27* off 10) launched a brutal assault in the death overs, propelling RCB to a mammoth 233/4.

Chasing a daunting target, LSG lost wickets at regular intervals. Shubman Gill fought valiantly with 46 off 24, and Abishek Porel played a gritty knock of 53. However, RCB's spinners dominated the proceedings. KA Maharaj was outstanding, claiming 3 wickets for just 22 runs, while Yash Singh also impressed with a 3-wicket haul. The asking rate climbed rapidly, and despite a late cameo from HH Pandya (37 off 21), LSG could only manage 194/8, falling short by 39 runs.

A comprehensive victory for RCB, powered by a dominant batting display and clinical spin bowling.
