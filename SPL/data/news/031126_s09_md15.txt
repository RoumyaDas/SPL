SPL S09 • QUALIFIER 01 • SRH vs MI • KKR

SRH won the toss and chose to bat on a track offering 3x spin assistance — but FH Allen had other ideas, launching a breathtaking 74 off 40 with 5 sixes to set the tone. VR Iyer's unbeaten 57 off 29 ensured SRH posted a daunting 202/7. Lamichhane and Shukla picked up 2 wickets apiece, while Starc endured a torrid night (0/56).

In reply, MI lost Owen for a duck, but Urvil Patel (76 off 41) and Abhishek Sharma (76 off 36)* produced a masterclass in chasing. Abhishek's 211.11 strike rate and five sixes sealed the deal with 13 balls to spare, sending MI into the final! 🔥
