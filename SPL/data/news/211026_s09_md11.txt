# 🏆 SPL S09 – Matchday 11: Complete Summary

Matchday 11 delivered 5 games, and every single one had a story worth telling. Here's how it all unfolded.

---

## Match 51 – SRH vs RCB (at SRH)
*Pitch: pace-friendly (1.6x more than spin)*

RCB won the toss and elected to bat — and what a decision it turned out to be. **Atharva Taide (86 off 55)** and **PWH de Silva (77 off 36)** tore into the SRH attack, with de Silva's 213.89 strike rate proving especially brutal. Abhishek Pathak's unbeaten 36 off 14 gave the innings a furious finish as RCB posted **228/3**. PJ Cummins (2/27) was the only SRH bowler with real control.

SRH's chase was built on **VR Iyer's astonishing 83* off 33** — 12 fours and 2 sixes at a strike rate of 251.52 — alongside FH Allen's 58 off 30. But the asking rate kept climbing, and despite Iyer's heroics, SRH finished **4 runs short at 225/5**. SS Ghosh's 3/41 was the difference-maker. **RCB won by 4 runs; VR Iyer took POTM** despite being on the losing side.

---

## Match 52 – PBKS vs RR (at PBKS)
*Pitch: pace-friendly (2.0x more than spin)*

RR won the toss and put PBKS in — a decision that backfired spectacularly. **V Suryavanshi (46 off 21)** set the tone, but it was **RK Singh's unbeaten 77 off 36** (5 fours, 5 sixes) that turned the innings into a monster. PBKS posted **206/7**, with Utkarsh Singh (2/22, 12 dots) the only RR bowler who escaped punishment.

The RR chase never got going. **R Ngarava (3/12 in 3.1 overs, 11 dots)** and **Shashank Singh (3/41)** ripped through the top and middle order, and only YBK Jaiswal (42 off 20) offered any real resistance. RR were bundled out for **109 in 15.1 overs** — a 97-run defeat. **PBKS won by 97 runs; RK Singh was POTM.**

---

## Match 53 – DC vs KKR (at DC)
*Pitch: pace-friendly (2.1x more than spin)*

KKR won the toss and chose to bat, and for a while it looked like a masterstroke. **YS Samra (52 off 33)** and **D Wiese (51 off 20 at a strike rate of 255)** powered KKR to **208/8**. But DC's bowlers kept chipping away — **Harsh Dubey (3/23)** and **JJ Bumrah (3/39)** were both excellent, and KKR's total felt maybe 15 runs short.

It was. **GJ Maxwell produced a masterclass — 103* off 57** with 11 fours and 2 sixes — and **SA Yadav (59 off 39)** gave him superb support. Tarang Gohel's quickfire 30 off 13 kept the momentum going, and DC cruised home at **211/4 in 19.2 overs**. **DC won by 6 wickets; GJ Maxwell was POTM.**

---

## Match 54 – GT vs CSK (at GT)
*Pitch: spin-friendly (2.0x more than pace)*

CSK won the toss and chose to bowl — and GT's openers made them pay early. **JP Inglis carried his bat for 90* off 47** (9 fours, 3 sixes), and **D Brevis bludgeoned 44* off just 21** at the death. GT posted **205/3**, with Hasan Ali (1/28) the pick of a CSK attack that otherwise struggled for control.

CSK's response was a statement. **RD Gaikwad was immovable — 112* off 59** with 12 fours and 2 sixes — and **Tilak Varma (48 off 30)** kept him company in a 134-run opening stand. KK Nair's 35 off 16 ensured there was no late wobble, and CSK chased it down with 11 balls to spare at **206/2**. **CSK won by 8 wickets; RD Gaikwad was POTM.**

---

## Match 55 – LSG vs MI (at LSG)
*Pitch: pace-friendly (2.3x more than spin)*

LSG won the toss and batted — and MI's bowlers made it count. **Abhilash Shetty (3/25, 11 dots)** and **JR Hazlewood (2/28, 8 dots)** kept the lid on throughout, and despite **Sameer Rizvi's unbeaten 46 off 27** and Ajay Rohera's brisk 29 off 14, LSG could only manage **183/8**. HH Pandya (2/32) and Ashwin Das (2/29) would later prove that total was gettable.

MI's chase was the **Urvil Patel show**. His **94 off 41 balls** — 4 fours and 7 sixes at a strike rate of 229.27 — was a demolition job, and **Abhishek Sharma's 34 off 19** ensured there was no collapse when wickets fell around him. MI reached **184/7 in just 18 overs**. **MI won by 3 wickets; Urvil Patel was POTM.**
