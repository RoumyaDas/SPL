# 🏏 Match 68 – DC vs MI: A Run-Fest and a Record Chase

The stage was set for a high-octane clash at the DC, with the pitch offering slight assistance to pacers. MI won the toss and elected to bowl first, a decision that seemed questionable as DC's openers came out all guns blazing. GJ Maxwell and N Sindhu provided a blistering start, but MI fought back through Arshad Khan and JR Hazlewood, picking up crucial wickets. However, a late-order blitz from Sunny Sandhu and Gurnoor Brar, coupled with a fiery cameo from Adarsh Singh, propelled DC to a mammoth total of 220. The score seemed more than enough on a track that had something for the bowlers.

Chasing 221 for victory, MI's chase was nothing short of spectacular. Urvil Patel and Tushar Raheja laid a solid foundation, putting on a record-breaking opening stand. Tushar Raheja played a scintillating knock, while Urvil Patel anchored the innings with a fluent half-century. The introduction of Abhishek Sharma added further firepower, as he launched a brutal assault on the DC bowlers. The required run rate was never a concern as MI's batters toyed with the bowling attack, reaching the target with 3.3 overs to spare. It was a clinical chase that left the opposition with no answers. 🏆

**Winner:** MI  
**Player of the Match:** Urvil Patel
