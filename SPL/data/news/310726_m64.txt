# 🏏 SPL Season 08, Match 64 – MI vs RCB

A spin-dominated contest where MI defended a modest total with grit, outclassing RCB in a tense battle.

MI, batting first, lost early wickets but **Urvil Patel** anchored the innings with a classy 79 off 50 balls. **Rohan Kunnummal** provided the fireworks with 67 off 39, while **KA Maharaj** ran through the MI middle order with a sensational 4-wicket haul, restricting them to 198/6 — a par total on a tricky surface.

RCB's chase started brightly with **Atharva Taide** (33 off 18) and **Priyansh Arya** (12), but wickets tumbled in the middle overs. **PWH de Silva** played a lone warrior's knock, smashing 67 off 26 balls to keep RCB in the hunt, but **Naseem Shah** struck thrice and **Satyajeet Bachhav** picked two crucial wickets to derail the chase. RCB finished at 190/7, falling 8 runs short despite a late flourish.

A clinical bowling performance from MI, who held their nerve to defend a challenging total.
