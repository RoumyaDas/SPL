# SPL S09 — Matchday 12: Complete Recap

Matchday 12 delivered five games packed with drama — a 4-run thriller, a five-wicket haul, a 122-run masterclass, a 99* near-century, and a clinical chase. Here's the full rundown:

---

## Match 56 — RR vs DC (at RR_2)
**RR won by 4 runs**

RR posted a mammoth **233/3**, powered by a stunning **Aniket Verma 122 (54)** — 16 fours and 5 sixes — alongside **KA Pollard 56 (51)** and a late **YBK Jaiswal 14* (3)** cameo. DC's bowlers were taken apart, with **DS Airee (1/22)** the only one with respectable figures.

DC's chase was brave but fell agonizingly short at **229/7**. **SA Yadav 88 (46)** and **N Sindhu 55 (33)** kept them in it, with **Tarang Gohel 37* (11)** producing a late flurry, but **Karan Lal's 4/40** proved decisive. Aniket Verma's century earned him POTM in a thriller.

---

## Match 57 — GT vs LSG (at GT)
**GT won by 8 wickets (20 balls to spare)**

LSG posted **191/9** via **Shubman Gill 70 (36)** and **BJ Bennett 46 (39)**. **GF Linde (4/39)** and **Sufiyan Muqeem (2/27)** starred with the ball.

GT cruised home at **192/2 in 16.4 overs** through **Naman Dhir 95* (50)** and **JP Inglis 81 (43)**. Naman Dhir took POTM.

---

## Match 58 — CSK vs RCB (at CSK_2)
**RCB won by 4 wickets (14 balls remaining)**

CSK were restricted to **187/7** despite **Tilak Varma 65 (39)** and **RD Gaikwad 55 (36)**. The story was **SS Ghosh's 5/46** — a five-wicket haul that ripped through the middle order, with **KA Maharaj (1/24)** and **DR Briggs (1/40)** chipping in.

RCB's chase was anchored by **PWH de Silva 62 (29)** and **Atharva Taide 53 (29)**, with **Harnoor Singh 28 (19)** and a late **S Gill 10* (3)** finish. **RE van der Merwe (3/38)** and **Hasan Ali (2/30)** threatened, but RCB reached **191/6 in 17.4 overs**. SS Ghosh took POTM for his five-for.

---

## Match 59 — KKR vs PBKS (at KKR)
**KKR won by 2 wickets (5 balls remaining)**

PBKS posted **185/7** via **RK Singh 59 (30)** and **Vansh Bedi 37 (20)**. **SO Ngoche (3/41)** and **Shahbaz Ahmed (2/47)** kept it tight.

KKR chased it down at **187/8 in 19.1 overs** through **Ishan Kishan 73 (42)** and **Tushar Raheja 51 (24)**. **R Ashwin (3/30)** and **CV Varun (2/45)** made it nervy. Ishan Kishan took POTM.

---

## Match 60 — MI vs SRH (at MI)
**SRH won by 9 wickets (4 balls remaining)**

MI posted **194/8** via **Abhishek Sharma 51 (26)** and **WL Madsen 48 (29)**. **A Zampa (3/33)** and **PJ Cummins (2/42)** were the pick.

SRH romped home at **198/1 in 19.2 overs** through **P Simran Singh 99* (50)** and **SU Parakh 73 (57)**. Simran Singh took POTM.
