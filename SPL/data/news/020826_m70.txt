# 🏏 Match 70 – LSG vs RR: A Bowling Masterclass That Demolished the Chase

The stage was set for a high-octane clash at LSG, with the pitch offering significant assistance to pacers. LSG won the toss and elected to bat first, a decision that seemed justified as they got off to a steady start. However, wickets at regular intervals kept the RR bowlers in the game. It was the magnificent partnership between Abishek Porel and HH Pandya that turned the tide. Porel played a sublime anchor innings, while Pandya unleashed a brutal assault in the death overs, smashing 29 runs in the 19th over alone. Their explosive partnership propelled LSG to a commanding total of 217, setting up what seemed like an unassailable target.

Chasing 218 for victory, RR's innings was over before it even began. Mohammad Amir produced a spell of pure magic, dismantling the top order with his fiery pace and precision. He picked up a five-wicket haul, leaving RR reeling at 14 for 4. Ashwin Das complemented him brilliantly, running through the middle and lower order with his clever variations. KA Pollard fought a lone battle with a valiant half-century, but lacked any support from the other end. The required run rate kept climbing, and wickets continued to tumble as RR were bundled out for a paltry 99. It was a clinical bowling performance that left the opposition with no answers. 🏆

**Winner:** LSG  
**Player of the Match:** Mohammad Amir
