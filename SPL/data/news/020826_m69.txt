# 🏏 Match 69 – KKR vs PBKS: A Spin Web That Strangled the Chase

The stage was set for a fascinating contest at KKR, with the pitch offering significant assistance to spinners. KKR won the toss and elected to bat first, but their innings never quite got going. Ishan Kishan fell early, and wickets tumbled at regular intervals. TM Head anchored the innings with a gritty unbeaten 72, but lacked support from the other end. Anmolpreet Singh provided some resistance with a well-made 48, but a flurry of wickets in the death overs meant KKR could only manage a modest total of 158. It seemed under par on a tricky surface.

Chasing 159, PBKS started positively with TL Seifert and J Suresh Kumar putting on a steady opening stand. However, the introduction of spin turned the tide dramatically. B Kumar and Shahbaz Ahmed bowled with incredible control, squeezing the life out of the chase. Wickets fell in clusters as PBKS's middle order crumbled under pressure. Despite a valiant 81 from TL Seifert, he ran out of partners as the required rate climbed. The KKR spinners wove a web that proved too difficult to escape, bowling out PBKS for just 132 and securing a comfortable victory. 🏆

**Winner:** KKR  
**Player of the Match:** JC Archer
