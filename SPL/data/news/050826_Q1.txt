**🏏 SPL S08 – QUALIFIER 1: KKR vs SRH – A HEIST FOR THE AGES**

The stage was set for a classic, and boy, did it deliver. In a high-octane Qualifier 1, SRH chased down a mammoth 206 with the coolness of a seasoned heist crew.

After being put in to bat, KKR's innings was a rollercoaster of fireworks and collapses. Ishan Kishan and TM Head provided a swift start, but wickets tumbled in clusters. Just when it seemed KKR might stall, Jay Gohil played a masterclass, anchoring the innings with an unbeaten 75 off just 36 balls. His calculated assault, along with cameos from WL Madsen and C Green, dragged KKR to a competitive 205/7, a total that looked imposing on the board.

But SRH had other plans. The chase began with a bang from P Simran Singh, but it was the magnificent Ben Duckett who orchestrated the chase with a near-flawless 91 off 50 balls. He found an able partner in the ever-reliable KL Rahul, who added a steady 39. The equation seemed to tighten as the death overs approached, but Kartik Sharma walked in and turned the game on its head. He demolished the bowling attack with an explosive 31* off just 15 balls, smashing three massive sixes to snatch victory from KKR's grasp.

It was a tactical and nerve-shattering battle that showcased the very best of SPL cricket. In the end, SRH emerged victorious by 6 wickets with 4 balls to spare, riding on Duckett's brilliance and Kartik's final flourish.

**Winner:** SRH (Won by 6 Wickets)
**Player of the Match:** BM Duckett (SRH)
