**🏏 SPL S08 – THE FINAL: SRH vs KKR – A SPIN MASTERCLASS CROWNS SRH CHAMPIONS!**

The grand finale of SPL Season 08 delivered a spectacle of spin bowling that left a packed house in awe. On a pitch tailor-made for spinners, SRH outclassed KKR to lift the coveted trophy in stunning fashion.

Winning the toss and batting first, SRH's innings was a tale of two halves. KL Rahul, in imperious form, smashed a breathtaking 63 off just 27 balls, while Kartik Sharma provided the late fireworks with a rapid 52 off 23, including five towering sixes. But KKR's spinners struck back with venom – Shahbaz Ahmed produced a sensational spell of 4/26, and V Puthur chipped in with 3 crucial wickets to bundle out SRH for 184 in just 17.2 overs. It seemed KKR had the upper hand.

But the chase turned into a nightmare for KKR. Adam Zampa, the veteran magician, weaved his web with devastating effect, claiming 4 wickets for just 31 runs. He was ably supported by M Markande, who grabbed 3 scalps, as SRH's spin trio completely dismantled KKR's formidable batting line-up. From Ishan Kishan's early fireworks to a late-order collapse, KKR never found their footing. They were skittled out for a paltry 153 in just 17 overs, handing SRH a comprehensive victory.

It was a final that showcased the beauty and brutality of spin bowling. SRH, with their tactical brilliance and clinical execution, proved they were the deserving champions of SPL Season 08!

**Winner:** SRH (Won by 31 Runs)  
**Player of the Match:** A Zampa (SRH)
