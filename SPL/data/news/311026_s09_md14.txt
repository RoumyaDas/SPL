# SPL S09 – Matchday 14 Summary

The final matchday of group stage of SPL S09 delivered games with plenty of drama, a couple of one-sided chases, and one thriller that went down to the wire. Here's how each game unfolded:

---

## Match 66: MI vs RCB (Venue: MI)

**Pitch:** Spin-friendly (2.0x more than pace). MI won the toss and chose to bowl.

RCB's innings never really got going against MI's spinners. Washington Sundar fell for a 6-ball duck, and despite SR Dickson's 44 (29) and a late blitz from Abhishek Tanwar (48* off 19), RCB crawled to 162/6. Shivam Shukla (2/14) and Prashant Solanki (2/18) were outstanding, conceding almost nothing through the middle.

The chase was a formality. MI's openers came out swinging — Urvil Patel smashed 16 off 6 before falling, but Akshat Raghuwanshi (29 off 13) and Jay Gohil (44 off 17) kept the tempo high. MI raced to 163/6 in just 13 overs, winning by 4 wickets with 42 balls to spare. Abhishek Tanwar's late cameo earned him POTM despite being on the losing side.

---

## Match 67: KKR vs RR (Venue: KKR)

**Pitch:** Strongly pace-friendly (4.0x more than spin). KKR won the toss and batted.

KKR posted a competitive 174, built almost entirely around TM Head's 79 (48). The rest of the lineup struggled against A Nagwaswalla, who produced a stunning 4/16 spell, and Utkarsh Singh (2/27). KKR collapsed from 146/5 to 174 all out, losing their last five wickets for just 28 runs.

RR's chase looked on track at 98/2, with R Smaran anchoring beautifully (71 off 56) and KA Pollard contributing 38. But B Kumar (3/27) and MA Wood (1/14) applied the squeeze, and the required rate climbed. RR finished 10 runs short at 164/6, falling agonizingly short despite Smaran's heroics. Nagwaswalla's four-wicket haul was the difference and earned him POTM.

---

## Match 68: GT vs SRH (Venue: GT)

**Pitch:** Evenly balanced between spin and pace. SRH won the toss and bowled.

GT produced the batting performance of the day, posting 212/6. After a shaky start (30/2), D Brevis (34 off 21) and R Parag (30 off 15) steadied things before B Aparajith played a blinder — 54* off 29 — and C Connolly chipped in with 25* off 13. SRH's bowlers had no answers in the death overs.

SRH's chase never found momentum. Sufiyan Muqeem (4/27) tore through the top and middle order, and B Aparajith capped a dream match with 3/46. Only KH Pandya (32) and Vidyadhar Patil (31) offered resistance as SRH were bowled out for 172 in 18.2 overs, losing by 40 runs. Aparajith was the obvious POTM for his all-round heroics.

---

## Match 69: PBKS vs LSG (Venue: PBKS_2)

**Pitch:** Pace-friendly (2.0x more than spin). PBKS won the toss and bowled.

LSG's innings was a tale of two collapses. They lost Kohli and Gous to ducks in the first over, recovered through Ajay Rohera's 53 (33) and Sameer Rizvi's brilliant 73* (35), but BA Raine (4/32) and R Ngarava (2/15) kept striking at key moments. LSG finished on 169/8 — a total that felt 15-20 runs short.

PBKS made light work of the chase thanks to PD Salt's phenomenal 78 off just 33 balls (7 fours, 6 sixes). He and Shashank Singh (26) put the game to bed before RK Singh (27*) finished things off. PBKS reached 170/5 in 16.5 overs, winning by 5 wickets. Salt was the undisputed POTM.

---

## Match 70: DC vs CSK (Venue: DC)

**Pitch:** Strongly pace-friendly (4.0x more than spin). DC won the toss and batted.

DC's innings was a disaster from start to finish. Only Adarsh Singh (42 off 39) and J Fraser-McGurk (31 off 21) showed any resistance as Hasan Ali (2/19), Sonu Yadav (2/19), and Arshad Khan (2/25) ripped through the lineup. DC were bowled out for a paltry 135 in 17.4 overs — no milestones, no partnerships, no momentum.

CSK's chase was ruthless. PI Walter (50* off 33) and KK Nair (49* off 25) demolished the DC attack, with Tilak Varma contributing 33 before a run-out. CSK reached 136/1 in just 13.1 overs, winning by 9 wickets. Walter's unbeaten half-century and a run-out earned him POTM honours.

---

## Matchday 14 – Key Takeaways

- **Biggest win:** CSK's 9-wicket demolition of DC with 41 balls to spare.
- **Closest game:** KKR vs RR — RR fell 10 runs short in a tense finish.
- **Best individual performance:** B Aparajith (GT) — 54* (29) & 3/46 — a genuine all-round masterclass.
- **Best bowling spell:** A Nagwaswalla (RR) — 4/16 — nearly won RR the game single-handedly.
- **Best batting display:** PD Salt (PBKS) — 78 off 33 — pure carnage.
- **POTM winners:** Abhishek Tanwar (RCB), A Nagwaswalla (RR), B Aparajith (GT), PD Salt (PBKS), PI Walter (CSK).

And with that, the final matchday of SPL S09 group stage comes to a close — 5 games, 5 winners, and plenty of memories. 🏏🔥
