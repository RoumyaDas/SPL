🏏 **SPL S08 – Match 57 | KKR vs SRH – A Run-Fest with a Clinical Finish**

The pitch at KKR was a batter's paradise, and KKR's openers came out swinging like they had a point to prove. Ishan Kishan gave them a fiery start before falling early, but **TM Head** and **WL Madsen** took center stage, stitching together a partnership that was pure poetry in motion. Head played a majestic knock of 82 off just 45 balls, finding the rope at will, while Madsen anchored the innings with a composed 63 off 46. The late-order fireworks came from Anmolpreet Singh, who smashed a blistering 39 off only 12 balls, pushing KKR to a colossal **214/7**. SRH's bowlers had a mixed day—Rasikh Salam and NT Ellis picked up crucial wickets but bled runs, while WG Jacks had a day to forget, going for 57 in his 4 overs.

Chasing 215, SRH knew they had to go big from ball one, and they started reasonably well with P Simran Singh and MP Stoinis putting up a steady opening stand. But it was **KL Rahul** and **Kartik Sharma** who turned the chase into a nail-biter. Rahul smashed a breathtaking 47 off 21 balls, while Kartik showed why he's in red-hot form with a whirlwind 53 off 23, including four massive sixes. However, KKR's bowling had a trump card—**B Kumar**. The veteran pacer produced a masterclass in death bowling, picking up a sensational 4 wickets and conceding just 26 runs in his 4 overs. He removed Kartik, VR Iyer, WG Jacks, and R Shepherd in a devastating spell that derailed the chase. Despite Karan Shinde's late cameo, SRH fell agonizingly short at **202/7**, handing KKR a thrilling 12-run victory. B Kumar's precision under pressure earned him the Player of the Match and sealed a memorable win for KKR in a high-scoring thriller.
