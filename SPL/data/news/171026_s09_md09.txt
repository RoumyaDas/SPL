**SPL S09 – Matchday 09 Summary**

Matchday 09 delivered five games with plenty of drama, a century, a last-over finish, and some dominant bowling displays. Here's how each match unfolded.

---

**Match 41 – DC vs GT**
DC won the toss and elected to bat on a surface offering 4x assistance to spin, and it proved a fatal decision. N Sindhu fell for a duck in the opening over, caught by R Parag off M Siddharth, and despite a breezy 26 off 13 from GJ Maxwell and a fluent 37 off 19 from Tarang Gohel, DC never found sustained momentum. Sufiyan Muqeem was simply unplayable, claiming 4/21 in his 3 overs, while C Connolly (2/29) and M Siddharth (2/51) chipped in. Only SA Yadav offered real resistance with an unbeaten 56 off 28, but wickets tumbled around him — AD Russell managed just 4, DS Airee 7, and the tail folded meekly. DC were bowled out for a paltry 170 in 17.2 overs, a total that always looked 30-40 runs short.

In reply, GT paced their chase perfectly. SV Samson (49 off 31) and JP Inglis (54 off 37) put on a composed 91-run opening stand, neutralizing DC's spinners with smart rotation and selective aggression. AK Markram eventually broke through, having Samson caught by DS Airee, and Parth Chandan trapped Naman Dhir LBW for a quickfire 19. But R Parag (24* off 16) and D Brevis (25* off 12) ensured there were no late jitters, finishing the job with 17 balls to spare. GT won by 7 wickets, with Sufiyan Muqeem's four-wicket haul earning him the Player of the Match award.

---

**Match 42 – KKR vs LSG**
LSG won the toss and chose to bat on a pitch assisting spin. V Kohli produced a masterclass, carrying his bat for 120* off 61 balls with 8 sixes, while T Dahiya gave excellent support with 61* off 34. The pair added an unbroken 151-run stand as LSG posted 213/2. KKR's bowlers, particularly YS Chahal and SO Ngoche, took a wicket each but conceded heavily. In the chase, Tushar Raheja gave KKR a flying start with 55 off 24, but wickets fell regularly in the middle. C Green (39 off 23) and Abdul Samad (28 off 19) fought hard, but DR Mousley's 3/31 broke the back of the chase. KKR finished 177/9, falling short by 36 runs. Kohli's century earned him the Player of the Match award.

---

**Match 43 – RCB vs PBKS**
RCB won the toss and bowled first. PBKS lost TL Seifert for a duck early, but V Suryavanshi (45 off 22) and Vansh Bedi (64 off 38) put together a strong platform. However, the middle order stuttered, and only some late hitting from RK Singh (25* off 16) and JJ Smit (15* off 9) pushed PBKS to 188/7. SS Ghosh and S Gill took two wickets each for RCB. In reply, RCB lost Atharva Taide early, but CH Gayle (49 off 27) and Priyansh Arya (91* off 42) took the game away with a dominant partnership. Arya's unbeaten knock, featuring 8 fours and 4 sixes, ensured RCB cruised home in just 17.4 overs, winning by 8 wickets. Priyansh Arya was named Player of the Match.

---

**Match 44 – SRH vs RR**
RR won the toss and bowled first. SRH got off to a brisk start through FH Allen (32 off 15), but wickets tumbled regularly after that. KL Rahul held the innings together with 71 off 44, but lacked support as SM Curran (4/47) and C Sakariya (3/36) tore through the lineup. SRH were bowled out for 186 in 19.5 overs. In reply, RR lost Aniket Verma early, but WG Jacks played a superb anchor innings of 78 off 50, and KA Pollard contributed 30 off 33. The finish was dramatic — GD Phillips unleashed a stunning 38* off just 13 balls, including a six to seal the game in the 19th over. RR won by 6 wickets with 5 balls remaining. WG Jacks was the Player of the Match.

---

**Match 45 – MI vs CSK**
MI won the toss and chose to bowl on a pace-friendly surface, and the decision looked questionable early as CSK's openers took full advantage. PI Walter (44 off 32) and Tilak Varma (52 off 30) added 85 for the first wicket, with Tilak in particularly punishing form. MA Starc was the one bowler who consistently threatened, and he broke the stand by bowling Tilak in the 15th over. KK Nair (19 off 13) and RD Gaikwad (47 off 21) kept the momentum going, and T Stubbs contributed an unbeaten 25 off 15. Starc finished with 3/31, while Sunil Kumar (2/51) and MW Short (1/33) picked up the others. CSK posted a formidable 211/7.

MI's chase got off to a disastrous start when MJ Owen fell for a second-ball duck, caught by Tilak Varma off Hasan Ali. But that brought WL Madsen to the crease, and what followed was one of the innings of the season. Madsen tore into the CSK attack, racing to a 63-ball 111 with 13 fours and 3 sixes, dominating partnerships with Urvil Patel (73 off 41) and Abhishek Sharma (18* off 7). M Pathirana bowled with pace and heart, picking up 2/44, but the target was simply too modest for a batter in Madsen's mood. MI romped home in 18.5 overs, winning by 7 wickets. Madsen's century made him the obvious Player of the Match.

---

**Matchday 09 Roundup:** GT, LSG, RCB, RR, and MI all picked up crucial wins. V Kohli's unbeaten 120 was the standout individual performance, while Sufiyan Muqeem, Priyansh Arya, WG Jacks, and WL Madsen all delivered match-winning contributions for their respective sides. The race for the playoffs continues to heat up.
