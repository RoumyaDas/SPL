🏏 **SPL S08 – Match 60 | RCB vs PBKS – A Chase for the Ages**

The RCB pitch had pace on offer, and PBKS made full use of it early on. Openers **V Suryavanshi** and **TL Seifert** came out with intent, smashing boundaries at will. Suryavanshi played a breathtaking knock of 44 off just 19 balls before falling to Chirag Jani, but the real show was put on by **N Rana**, who played one of the innings of the season—an unbeaten 75 off 36 balls, studded with 5 towering sixes and 6 boundaries. He anchored the innings beautifully, even as wickets tumbled around him. RCB's bowlers fought back brilliantly in the middle overs, with **Chirag Jani** and **HJ Manenti** picking up 3 wickets each, but PBKS still managed to post a formidable **187**, thanks to N Rana's late fireworks.

Chasing 188, RCB openers **Atharva Taide** and **Priyansh Arya** put on a batting masterclass. They stitched together a magnificent 130-run partnership that laid the perfect foundation. Taide scored a composed 65 off 43, while Arya was at his explosive best, hammering 68 off 39 balls with 9 boundaries. Even after a mini-collapse that saw them lose 4 quick wickets, **JM Sharma** walked in and finished the game in style, smashing an unbeaten 21 off just 8 balls, while **PWH de Silva** provided the finishing touches with a quickfire 21 off 9. RCB chased down the target in just 18 overs, winning by 6 wickets in a thrilling run-fest. N Rana's spectacular knock earned him the Player of the Match, but it was RCB's collective batting firepower that stole the show.
