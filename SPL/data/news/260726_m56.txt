🏏 **SPL S08 – Match 56 | RR vs DC – A Tale of Two Halves**

The stage was set, the pitch had pace, and RR opted to bat first—a decision that looked like a masterstroke inside the Powerplay. Aniket Verma came out with fireworks, smashing 29 off 15, but it was the towering presence of **S Dube** that truly anchored RR’s innings. He played a calm yet brutal knock of 77 off 52, holding the middle together while wickets tumbled around him. Kumar Kushagra provided a cheeky 40-run cameo, while GD Phillips came in and went berserk with a blistering 40 off just 18 balls. RR’s total of **191/7** felt massive, especially after a chaotic final over that saw 3 wickets fall in quick succession. But it was the bowling of DC—particularly **JJ Bumrah**—that kept things in check for most parts, with Bumrah picking two crucial wickets and bowling with elite control. Easwaran K and MJ Owen chipped in as well, but the total looked like a tough mountain to climb for the chasing side.

If the first innings was a symphony of power-hitting, the second was a nerve-shredding thriller. DC’s chase began with a hurricane start from **GJ Maxwell**, who smashed 24 off just 13 balls before falling to a sharp LBW trap laid by RR’s bowling spearhead. Enter **A Nagwaswalla**, who turned the tide single-handedly. With a fiery spell of 4 wickets, he dismantled DC’s top and middle order—removing Maxwell, JJ Bumrah, Shaheen Afridi, and the dangerous Adarsh Singh, who was fighting a lone battle with a gritty 38. For a moment, **AD Russell** looked like he might pull off a heist, smashing a composed 42 off 33, but the mounting required run-rate and lack of support from the other end proved too steep. Despite a late six from Easwaran K, DC fell short at **173/9**, handing RR a thrilling 18-run victory. Nagwaswalla’s all-round brilliance was the difference, as he not only took wickets but kept the pressure on from the very first over, earning him a well-deserved Player of the Match award.
