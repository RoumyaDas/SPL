🏏 **SPL S08 – Match 58 | GT vs MI – A Chase That Had Everything**

The GT pitch offered something for the spinners, but MI's bowlers had to work hard to contain a dangerous GT batting lineup. Openers **SV Samson** and **JP Inglis** came out all guns blazing, putting on 46 runs in just 3.4 overs before Shivam Shukla broke through. Inglis fell for a quick 26, and Samson followed soon after for 25, but **Naman Dhir** held the innings together with a steady 27 off 20. The middle order struggled against MI's disciplined spin attack—**Shivam Shukla** and **Sikandar Raza** picked up two wickets each, while **JR Hazlewood** and **Akshya Deka** chipped in with crucial breakthroughs. However, **V Nigam** (35 off 19) and **AR Patel** (33* off 24) pushed GT to a competitive **190/7**, with some late fireworks from Ashutosh Sharma (14 off 9). It was a solid total, but the pitch was starting to ease out.

Chasing 191, MI openers **Urvil Patel** and **Tushar Raheja** came out with absolute intent. Raheja fell for a rapid 31, but Urvil Patel was unstoppable—he hammered 77 off just 35 balls, a knock that included 5 boundaries and 4 towering sixes. **Abhishek Sharma** joined the party with a composed 62 off 36, and the pair added 119 runs for the second wicket, utterly dismantling GT's bowling attack. GT's spinners fought back valiantly—**Sufiyan Muqeem** picked up 3 wickets and **Washington Sundar** grabbed 3 as well—but the damage was already done. MI chased down the target in just 17.3 overs, winning by 1 wicket in a nerve-shredding finish. Urvil Patel's breathtaking knock earned him the Player of the Match, and MI's chase will go down as one of the most clinical run-chases of the season.
