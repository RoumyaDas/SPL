# SPL S09 – Matchday 06 Summary

## The Big Picture

Matchday 06 delivered **five thrillers**, with four games going down to the wire and one absolute run-fest. **231, 212, 213, 222, 226** — the first-innings scores read like a batting carnival. Bowlers were largely reduced to spectators on flat decks, but a few special spells stood out. Notably, **teams batting first won only once** (CSK), while chasers dominated elsewhere. We also saw a **century, a five-wicket haul, and a 100* in a losing cause** — drama everywhere.

---

## Match 26 – CSK vs DC (Venue: CSK)

**CSK 231/5 (20.0) beat DC 226/10 (20.0) by 5 runs**

A near-perfect batting display from CSK, built on **KK Nair's stunning 97* (53)** and a brisk 37 from Dhruv Jurel. DC's bowlers were taken apart, with only Parth Chandan (1/17) and GJ Maxwell (1/21) escaping relatively unscathed. In reply, DC looked dead and buried at 96/2 before **GJ Maxwell exploded with 88 (46)** and **J Fraser-McGurk smoked 45 (25)**. But **M Pathirana produced a death-overs masterclass — 5/34 (4.0)** — to rip the heart out of DC's chase. Arshad Khan (2/43) provided able support. DC finished 226 all out, just 5 short. **POTM: M Pathirana.**

---

## Match 27 – SRH vs LSG (Venue: SRH)

**SRH 215/6 (19.4) beat LSG 212/7 (20.0) by 4 wickets**

LSG posted a formidable 212/7 thanks to a **breathtaking century from Sameer Rizvi — 100* (45)**, supported by DR Mousley's 41 (23). SRH's bowlers shared the wickets, with NT Ellis (2/42) and SP Narine (2/51) the pick. In the chase, **KL Rahul anchored beautifully with 86 (50)**, while P Simran Singh contributed 45 (32). LSG's Arshdeep Singh bowled his heart out for **3/51**, and JA Duffy chipped in with 2/50, but SRH got home with two balls to spare. A proper nail-biter. **POTM: Sameer Rizvi** (for his century, despite being on the losing side).

---

## Match 28 – RCB vs KKR (Venue: RCB)

**KKR 156/6 (18.5) beat RCB 154/10 (16.4) by 4 wickets**

The only low-scoring affair of the day — and it was all about **Shahbaz Ahmed**. The KKR spinner **ripped through RCB with 3/17 (3.0)**, ably supported by Pulkit Narang (2/12) and SO Ngoche (2/32). RCB were bundled out for 154 in just 16.4 overs, with JM Sharma's 34* (23) the only real resistance. In reply, **Ishan Kishan (45 off 28)** and **S Arora (43* off 26)** ensured KKR crossed the line comfortably despite Rashid Khan (2/22) and V Puthur (2/34) keeping things interesting. **POTM: Shahbaz Ahmed** — a true all-round match-winning performance.

---

## Match 29 – RR vs PBKS (Venue: RR)

**PBKS 214/6 (20.0) beat RR 213/8 (20.0) by 4 wickets**

Another last-ball thriller! RR posted 213/8 on the back of **YBK Jaiswal's blazing 64 (29)** and Aniket Verma's 39 (19). PBKS's Ravi Teja (2/34) and JJ Smit (2/38) led the bowling effort. The chase was all about **Vansh Bedi**, who played the innings of the match — **81 (45) with 7 fours and 5 sixes** — before falling to L Ngidi. **RK Singh then produced a stunning cameo — 45* (19)** — to seal the deal. C Sakariya (2/37) and A Nagwaswalla (1/31) were RR's best. **POTM: Vansh Bedi.**

---

## Match 30 – GT vs MI (Venue: GT)

**MI 226/3 (18.3) beat GT 222/4 (20.0) by 7 wickets**

The highest-scoring game of the day. GT posted 222/4 with **JP Inglis (71* off 37)** and **D Brevis (58 off 30)** in destructive form. MI's bowlers were expensive, with only JR Hazlewood (1/36) and Shivam Shukla (1/42) among the wickets. In reply, MI were simply unstoppable. **Abhishek Sharma played a breathtaking knock — 87* (45) with 6 fours and 5 sixes** — and **Jay Gohil matched him stroke for stroke with 63* (33)**. GT's bowlers had no answers, and MI chased it down with 9 balls to spare. **POTM: Abhishek Sharma.**

**Final Word:** Matchday 06 was a batting festival with just enough bowling magic to keep things spicy. Pathirana's five-for and Shahbaz's all-round show were the standout bowling performances, while Sameer Rizvi's century and Abhishek Sharma's unbeaten 87 were the innings of the day. The chases ruled — and the fans were the real winners. 🔥🏏
