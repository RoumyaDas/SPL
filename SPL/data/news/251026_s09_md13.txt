# SPL S09 – Matchday 13: Complete Summary

Matchday 13 delivered five games of wildly contrasting drama — a last-ball thriller, a one-sided demolition, a statement chase, a five-wicket haul in a losing cause, and a century that lit up the round.

---

## Match 61: CSK vs KKR (at CSK)

**Toss:** CSK won, elected to bowl first.

KKR were put in and never truly got going. Early wickets tumbled — TM Head bowled by M Pathirana for 2, Tushar Raheja caught for 9 — before MR Marsh briefly exploded with 29 off 14. But the innings belonged to **Abdul Samad**, who held it together with 63 off 46 (5 fours, 2 sixes), well supported by D Wiese (20) and Akhil Singh Rawat (19). **Geet Puri** was the pick of the CSK attack with 3/24, while Arshad Khan (2/19) and Hasan Ali (2/33) chipped in. A late cameo from Imran Tahir (11* off 6) lifted KKR to **170 all out in 19.1 overs**.

The chase was a procession. RD Gaikwad fell for 18 and Tilak Varma was run out for 3, but that was as good as it got for KKR. **KK Nair (66* off 43)** and **T Stubbs (85* off 47, 7 fours, 4 sixes)** put on an unbroken match-winning stand, dismantling every KKR bowler — Shahbaz Ahmed went for 51, MP Yadav for 38. CSK cruised home at **176/2 in 17.4 overs**.

**Winner:** CSK (8 wickets, 14 balls to spare) | **POTM:** T Stubbs (CSK)

---

## Match 62: LSG vs SRH (at LSG_2)

**Toss:** SRH won, elected to bat first on a pace-friendly pitch (4.0x pace assistance).

SRH's innings was a tale of two partnerships. After **Mohammad Amir** removed P Simran Singh for a second-ball duck, **FH Allen (66 off 45)** and **VR Iyer (58 off 37)** rebuilt with authority, taking SRH to 138/3. Then the wheels came off spectacularly. **Mohammad Amir produced a devastating 5-wicket haul (5/29)**, and **Arshdeep Singh claimed 4/39** as SRH collapsed from 154/3 to **171 all out in 20 overs** — losing their last seven wickets for just 17 runs. KH Pandya's 13 off 4 was the only late resistance.

LSG's chase never found momentum. Shubman Gill fell for 4, Ajay Rohera for 9, and despite **V Kohli's blistering 22 off 9** (2 fours, 2 sixes) and **LR Chethan's 35 off 20**, the innings crumbled under relentless pressure. **Tripuresh Singh (3/13 in 3 overs, 14 dots)** and **NT Ellis (3/19)** were relentless, while SP Narine chipped in with 2/21. LSG were bundled out for **120 in 16.4 overs**, handing SRH a comprehensive victory.

**Winner:** SRH (51 runs) | **POTM:** Mohammad Amir (LSG) — 5/29, a lone warrior in defeat

---

## Match 63: PBKS vs MI (at PBKS_2)

**Toss:** PBKS won, elected to bowl first on a pitch favouring pace (2.0x).

MI's innings was a rollercoaster. Urvil Patel blazed 21 off 11 before falling, and B Sai Sudharsan crawled to 17 off 21. **MJ Owen (35 off 33)** and **Akshat Raghuwanshi (52 off 28, 5 sixes)** provided the engine, but when Abhishek Sharma (5) and MW Short (2) fell in quick succession, MI were staring at 139/6. Enter **Praveen Dubey (25* off 9, 3 sixes)** and Satyajeet Bachhav (14* off 13) — their late assault lifted MI to **179/6**. R Ngarava (2/21) and BA Raine (2/39) were the pick of the PBKS bowlers.

The chase was the **V Suryavanshi show**. The opener annihilated MI's attack — 99 off just 46 balls, with 12 fours and 6 sixes — racing PBKS to 145/3 before falling one short of a century in the 15th over. Shashank Singh (19* off 21) and BA Raine (7* off 3, including the winning six) finished the job after a brief wobble where Mohammed Shami (2/56) removed RK Singh and AR Patel in the 18th over. PBKS reached **180/6 in 18 overs**.

**Winner:** PBKS (4 wickets, 12 balls to spare) | **POTM:** V Suryavanshi (PBKS) — 99 (46), 12 fours, 6 sixes

---

## Match 64: RCB vs GT (at RCB)

**Toss:** RCB won, elected to bowl first on a balanced surface.

This was the **JP Inglis masterclass**. The GT opener carried his bat for a magnificent **117* off 58 balls** (12 fours, 5 sixes), combining with SV Samson (48 off 33) for an opening stand of 88, then uniting with **Naman Dhir (53* off 29)** for an unbroken 137-run partnership. RCB's bowlers had no answers — SS Ghosh went for 58, HJ Manenti for 52. GT posted a formidable **225/1 in 20 overs**.

RCB's chase had moments — **Atharva Taide (37 off 21)** and **Abhishek Pathak (64* off 37, 6 fours, 3 sixes)** fought hard — but wickets fell at regular intervals. Priyansh Arya (16), Harnoor Singh (0), PWH de Silva (13), JM Sharma (15) and Chirag Jani (1) all fell cheaply as **GF Linde (4/46)** ran through the middle order. Faheem Ashraf (2/32) and Prince Yadav (1/24) kept the pressure on. RCB were bowled out for **196 in 20 overs**, 29 runs short.

**Winner:** GT (29 runs) | **POTM:** JP Inglis (GT) — 117* (58)

---

## Match 65: DC vs RR (at DC)

**Toss:** RR won, elected to bowl first on a surface with 4.0x pace assistance.

This was a **Suryakumar Yadav symphony**. GJ Maxwell set the tone with 33 off 14, but **SA Yadav's unbeaten 101 off 43 balls** (12 fours, 5 sixes) was the centrepiece — an innings of pure, unadulterated destruction. **N Sindhu (52 off 24)** provided the perfect foil, and Tarang Gohel (36 off 20) chipped in as DC piled on **241/6 in 20 overs**. A Nagwaswalla was the only RR bowler with any joy (3/53), while T Natarajan conceded 66.

RR's reply was nothing short of a **catastrophe**. **JC Archer produced a spell of 5/12 in 3 overs**, ripping the heart out of the top order, while **JJ Bumrah (1/9 in 2 overs, 8 dots)** and Harsh Dubey (1/7) suffocated the middle. KA Pollard (21 off 11) and Aniket Verma (19 off 10) briefly threatened, but the end came swiftly — RR were shot out for **88 in just 9 overs**. The 153-run margin was the most emphatic of the round.

**Winner:** DC (153 runs) | **POTM:** JC Archer (DC) — 5/12 (3.0)

---

## Matchday 13 – Key Takeaways

- **POTM roll call:** T Stubbs (CSK), Mohammad Amir (LSG — in a losing cause), V Suryavanshi (PBKS), JP Inglis (GT), JC Archer (DC).
- **Centuries:** SA Yadav (101* off 43) and JP Inglis (117* off 58) lit up the round; V Suryavanshi fell one short on 99.
- **Five-wicket hauls:** Mohammad Amir (5/29) and JC Archer (5/12) — one in vain, one in a demolition.
- **Biggest win:** DC's 153-run thrashing of RR.
- **Closest finish:** PBKS's 4-wicket win over MI with 12 balls to spare.
- **Standout partnership:** Inglis & Naman Dhir's unbroken 137 for GT; Nair & Stubbs' match-winning stand for CSK.
- **Bowling collapse of the round:** SRH losing 7 wickets for 17 runs; RR being bowled out for 88 inside 9 overs.
