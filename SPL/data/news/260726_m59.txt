🏏 **SPL S08 – Match 59 | CSK vs LSG – Spin Web and a Ruthless Chase**

The pitch at CSK was a turner, and LSG's spinners made it their fortress. After being put in to bat, CSK's innings never quite found rhythm—they lost JM Vince early, but **AM Rahane** gave them a blazing start with a 16-ball 35, smashing three sixes to inject some momentum. **Tilak Varma** held one end together with a gritty 46 off 37, but runs were hard to come by as LSG's spin trio of **H Ssenyondo**, **Kuldeep Yadav**, and Shubhang Hegde choked the scoring. Ssenyondo was the wrecker-in-chief, picking up a sensational 4 wickets while conceding just 32 runs in his 4 overs. Kuldeep Yadav was equally economical, taking 2 wickets for only 10 runs. A late cameo from Sonu Yadav (38 off 25) gave CSK a fighting total of **165/9**, but it always felt under-par on a pitch that was getting trickier by the over.

Chasing a modest 166, LSG started briskly but lost Shubman Gill early to a sharp LBW. However, **Ajay Rohera** (25 off 13) and **Abishek Porel** (34 off 19) kept the scoreboard ticking. The game turned when **T Dahiya** walked in and played a breathtaking knock—34 off just 14 balls, including two sixes and three fours, shifting the momentum decisively. **Sameer Rizvi** then took over, smashing a brutal 42 off 19, while **HH Pandya** finished things off with an unbeaten 19 as LSG chased down the target in just 15 overs, winning by 4 wickets. It was a clinical chase, powered by fearless hitting and a spin-bowling masterclass from H Ssenyondo, who rightly claimed the Player of the Match.
