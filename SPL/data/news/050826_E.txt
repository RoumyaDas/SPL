**🏏 SPL S08 – ELIMINATOR: LSG vs DC – A RECORD-BREAKING RUN-FEST**

In a breathtaking Eliminator that redefined T20 batting, DC chased down a colossal 232 with an innings of pure power and precision. The match was a relentless assault on the bowling attacks from both sides.

Batting first, LSG set the tone early with a monumental 98 from Shubman Gill, who looked destined for a century before falling just short. Ajay Rohera provided the perfect foil with a classy 69, as the pair added 167 for the first wicket. A late cameo from HH Pandya (18 off 4) propelled LSG to a daunting 231/4, a total that seemed insurmountable.

However, DC's chase was nothing short of spectacular. Despite losing early wickets, Tarang Gohel played the innings of his life, smashing a breathtaking century (102 off 50) that kept DC in the hunt. He was ably supported by N Sindhu, who contributed a solid 70, stitching a crucial 161-run partnership. The equation tightened late on, but Andre Russell and DS Airee finished the job with a flurry of massive sixes, snatching victory from the jaws of defeat.

It was a night of monstrous hits and shattered records, but DC held their nerve to win by 4 wickets with 5 balls to spare, proving that no target is too big when you have the firepower.

**Winner:** DC (Won by 4 Wickets)  
**Player of the Match:** Tarang Gohel (DC)
