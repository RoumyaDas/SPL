# SPL S09 – Matchday 08 Summary

Matchday 08 delivered five absolute thrillers, with games decided in the final overs, standout individual performances, and plenty of drama across both innings. Here's the complete rundown:

---

## Match 36: SRH vs CSK – SRH Wins by 28 Runs

**Venue:** SRH

CSK won the toss and elected to bowl first, but that decision backfired spectacularly as SRH posted a mammoth **233/4** in their 20 overs. The innings was built on a blistering unbeaten partnership between **VR Iyer (66* off 28)** and **SP Narine (44* off 19)**, who tore into the CSK bowling attack in the death overs. Earlier, **FH Allen (42 off 26)** and **AA Kulkarni (32 off 15)** provided momentum, while **KL Rahul (25 off 17)** got starts. For CSK, **Tanush Kotian (1/17)** and **Arshad Khan (1/36)** were the pick of the bowlers, but the rest leaked runs heavily, with **M Pathirana (1/59)** and **Geet Puri (0/56)** enduring tough evenings.

In reply, CSK came out swinging and looked dangerous at times, but the chase fell apart. **Tilak Varma** played a stunning lone hand, smashing **64 off just 25 balls (6 fours, 4 sixes)**, while **Kranthi Kumar (48 off 28)** and **Tanush Kotian (36 off 27)** fought hard. However, SRH's bowling attack proved too strong. **NT Ellis** was the destructor-in-chief, claiming a sensational **5-wicket haul (5/35)** , while **SP Narine (2/23)** and **Rasikh Salam (2/38)** provided excellent support. CSK were eventually bowled out for **205 in 18.5 overs**, handing SRH a comfortable 28-run victory. **NT Ellis** was rightly named Player of the Match for his match-winning spell.

---

## Match 40: LSG vs RCB – LSG Wins by 3 Runs

**Venue:** LSG

RCB won the toss and chose to bowl, and for much of the innings, it looked like a good decision. LSG were reduced to **20/2** early, with **V Kohli (11)** and **Shubman Gill (0)** both falling cheaply to **S Gill (2/41)**. But then **Ajay Rohera** played one of the innings of the matchday, smashing **85 off 46 balls (11 fours, 2 sixes)** to anchor the innings. **LR Chethan** provided excellent support with a composed **50 off 43**, and **Sameer Rizvi** exploded at the end with **40* off just 15 balls (4 fours, 3 sixes)** to lift LSG to **197/4**. For RCB, **S Gill (2/41)** and **SS Ghosh (2/42)** were the standout bowlers, while **KA Maharaj (0/34)** was economical with 12 dot balls.

The chase was a nail-biter. RCB lost **CH Gayle (56 off 44)** and **Atharva Taide (63 off 31)** after they had set a solid platform, and despite a late cameo from **Chirag Jani (16* off 6)** , they fell agonizingly short. **H Ssenyondo (2/23)** and **Arshdeep Singh (2/51)** were the key wicket-takers for LSG, while **Mohammad Amir (1/34)** and **Ashwin Das (1/44)** kept things tight. RCB finished on **194/7**, just 3 runs short. **Ajay Rohera** was named Player of the Match for his match-winning 85.

---

## Match 39: DC vs PBKS – PBKS Wins by 6 Wickets

**Venue:** DC

PBKS won the toss and put DC in to bat. DC's innings was powered by a sensational knock from **Tarang Gohel**, who smashed **80 off 42 balls (8 fours, 4 sixes)** . **GJ Maxwell (31 off 14)** got them off to a flyer, and **JC Buttler** anchored the middle with **38* off 21**. **N Sindhu (34 off 22)** also chipped in. For PBKS, **AR Ramjani** was the star with the ball, claiming **3/49**, while **JJ Smit (2/38)** and **Shashank Singh (2/20)** kept DC in check. DC posted a competitive **216/8**.

In reply, PBKS were absolutely relentless. **V Suryavanshi** set the tone with a blistering **57 off 25 (6 fours, 3 sixes)** , and **Vansh Bedi** smashed **41 off just 15 balls** before a run-out ended his cameo. **PD Salt (48 off 28)** kept the momentum going, and **Shashank Singh** finished the job in style with an unbeaten **58* off 24 (3 fours, 4 sixes)** . PBKS chased down the target in just **17.5 overs**, finishing on **222/4**. For DC, **DS Airee (2/30)** and **Harsh Dubey (1/14)** were the only bowlers who had any success. **Shashank Singh** was named Player of the Match for his explosive finishing knock.

---

## Match 38: RR vs GT – RR Wins by 4 Runs

**Venue:** RR

GT won the toss and elected to bowl first on a spin-friendly surface, and for a while, it looked like a masterstroke. RR were bowled out for **212 in 18.2 overs**, but not before **Aniket Verma** played a breathtaking innings of **63 off 28 (8 fours, 3 sixes)** . **S Dube** held the middle with **58 off 33**, and **WG Jacks (22 off 10)** and **YBK Jaiswal (28 off 7)** provided late fireworks. For GT, **Sufiyan Muqeem (2/23)** and **Fazalhaq Farooqi (2/39)** were excellent, while **GF Linde (2/51)** and **V Nigam (2/49)** picked up crucial wickets despite conceding runs.

The chase was a classic. GT lost early wickets, but **D Brevis** played a stunning counter-attacking innings, smashing **71 off 37 balls (2 fours, 6 sixes)** to keep his team in the hunt. **R Parag** fought till the end with **42* off 20**, and **V Nigam** gave a late scare with **24 off 8**. But RR's bowlers held their nerve. **Mohammad Ghazanfar** was the hero with **3/45**, while **Karan Lal (2/33)** and **SM Curran (1/47)** provided crucial breakthroughs. GT finished on **208/7**, just 4 runs short. **D Brevis** was named Player of the Match for his incredible knock under pressure.

---

## Match 37: MI vs KKR – MI Wins by 4 Wickets

**Venue:** MI

KKR won the toss and chose to bat first on a surface favouring spin, but they ran into a one-man demolition crew. **Satyajeet Bachhav** produced a magical spell, claiming **5/27** to rip through the KKR lineup. **TM Head** was the only batter who stood tall, smashing **83 off 51 balls (10 fours, 1 six)** , while **Sairaj Patil (32 off 19)** provided some late resistance. KKR were bowled out for **177 in 20 overs**, with **Shivam Shukla (2/38)** and **MJ Owen (1/35)** offering good support.

In reply, MI made light work of the chase thanks to a brutal innings from **MJ Owen**, who smashed **96 off 45 balls (8 fours, 6 sixes)** . **Urvil Patel** got them off to a flying start with **31 off 14**, and despite a mini-collapse in the middle overs, MI cruised home in just **15.5 overs**, finishing on **181/6**. For KKR, **Sairaj Patil (2/38)** and **Noor Ahmad (2/37)** were the pick of the bowlers, but the total was never going to be enough. **MJ Owen** was named Player of the Match for his match-winning knock.

---

- **Best Batting Performance:** MJ Owen (96 off 45) – MI vs KKR
- **Best Bowling Performance:** NT Ellis (5/35) – SRH vs CSK & Satyajeet Bachhav (5/27) – MI vs KKR
- **Closest Finish:** LSG vs RCB (3 runs)
- **Highest Team Total:** SRH – 233/4
- **Most Dramatic Finish:** RR vs GT (4 runs)

Matchday 08 had everything – centuries narrowly missed, five-wicket hauls, last-over thrillers, and individual brilliance that turned games on their heads. The race for the playoffs is heating up!
