# 🏏 SPL Season 08, Match 61 – DC vs GT

A run-glutton's paradise! This was a record-breaking slugfest where bowlers were treated like bowling machines and batsmen turned into executioners.

DC, batting first, lost their way in the middle overs after a blazing start from **GJ Maxwell** (57 off 23) and **Ritik Vats** (40 off 18). At 138/5, things looked shaky. Then came **JC Buttler**, who played one of the most destructive knocks of the season — an unbeaten 107 off just 39 balls, featuring 10 fours and 7 sixes. He single-handedly propelled DC to a monstrous 293/6, the highest total of the tournament.

Chasing 294, GT came out swinging. **JP Inglis** played a captain's knock, smashing 109* off 52 balls, while **Naman Dhir** launched a brutal assault with 97 off 43. The duo added 194 runs for the third wicket, bringing the equation down to 39 runs needed off the final over. But the mountain was just too steep. GT finished at 273/2, falling 20 runs short in the most thrilling high-scoring chase of the season.

A match that rewrote record books, but DC held their nerve to defend the biggest total.
