**# 🏏 SPL S09 - MATCHDAY 01 ROUNDUP 🏏**

**WHAT A START TO THE SEASON!** Matchday 01 delivered everything we could have hoped for - last-ball thrillers, explosive batting displays, and bowling masterclasses that left fans on the edge of their seats!

---

## 🔥 MATCH 1: SRH vs KKR
**SRH WINS BY 4 WICKETS**

The day began with SRH showcasing why they're a force to be reckoned with. After restricting KKR to a below-par 173/7, the chase looked straightforward - until it wasn't! **FH Allen** announced himself with a blistering 63 off just 36 balls, but when he fell, the middle order wobbled. Enter **Karan Shinde** and **SP Narine**, who steadied the ship with a composed partnership. The finishing touches came from an unlikely hero - **Tripuresh Singh** smashing the winning boundary to seal a 4-wicket victory with 12 balls to spare. **Shahbaz Ahmed** was the architect of the bowling effort with a brilliant 3-wicket haul.

---

## 💥 MATCH 2: DC vs LSG
**DC WINS BY 2 WICKETS**

This was the **Sameer Rizvi show**! The DC batter played one of the most outrageous innings you'll ever see - **90 runs off just 40 balls** with 10 fours and 3 sixes, single-handedly dragging his team to a massive 209/5. LSG's chase was built on **V Kohli's** classy 48 and **AD Russell's** explosive 40, but they kept losing wickets at crucial moments. In the end, it came down to **Parth Chandan** holding his nerve, finishing unbeaten on 46 off 20 balls to get DC home with 2 balls remaining. Rizvi's heroics earned him a well-deserved Player of the Match award.

---

## ⚡ MATCH 3: GT vs RCB
**GT WINS BY 7 WICKETS**

Chasing 191 at GT is never easy, but **SV Samson** made it look like a walk in the park. The GT opener carried his bat through the innings with a magnificent **99* off 58 balls**, guiding his team home with 5 balls to spare. The support came from **D Brevis** (44 off 36) and **R Parag** (23* off 9), who finished things off in style. RCB's innings was built on contributions from **Abhishek Pathak** (32) and **JM Sharma** (35*), but they never quite got the momentum they needed. Samson's masterclass earned him the POTM award.

---

## 🎯 MATCH 4: MI vs RR
**RR WINS BY 7 WICKETS**

The penultimate match of the day was an absolute **run-fest**! MI posted a commanding 215/7 thanks to **Urvil Patel's** fluent 68 and **Akshat Raghuwanshi's** explosive 63 off just 34 balls. But RR had other ideas. **WG Jacks** played the innings of the day - an unbeaten **95 off 49 balls** - combining power with elegance. He found the perfect partner in **KA Pollard**, who rolled back the years with a brutal 69 off 43. The duo dismantled MI's bowling attack, chasing down the target with 3 balls to spare. Jacks was the obvious choice for Player of the Match.

---

# 🏏 MATCH 5: PBKS vs CSK
**PBKS WINS BY 43 RUNS**

The final game of Matchday 01 belonged to PBKS! After choosing to bat first, they were powered by **V Suryavanshi's** explosive 63 off just 29 balls — 10 fours and pure dominance. **Shashank Singh** (40* off 19) and **AR Patel** (26* off 7) then turned the death overs into a nightmare for CSK, lifting PBKS to **209/6**.

CSK's chase never really got going. **KK Nair** fought hard with a 27-ball 50, and **Tilak Varma** made 31, but PBKS kept taking wickets at regular intervals. **CV Varun** and **HV Patel** each claimed 3 wickets, while **Prashant Solanki** was the standout for CSK with 3/23. In the end, CSK were bowled out for **166 in 19.2 overs**.

**Player of the Match:** V Suryavanshi (PBKS) – 63 (29), 4s:10
